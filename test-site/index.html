Detection Test Bench

A fake site for testing Aliases for Fastmail. Nothing here is sent anywhere. A check passes when the badge appears (or stays away) as expected. The page reads the data-fmalias="attached" attribute the extension puts on the field that has the badge.

Should get the badge

1. Classic signup

Email is autofocused; confirm email, password and a "Create account" button sit in the same form.

Expected: badge on Email and on Confirm email (only the field you're in shows it).

2. Text field named "email"

type="text", name="email", placeholder "Your email address". Scores 2 (name) + 1 (placeholder) = 3.

Expected: badge.

3. React-style controlled field

Keeps its own state from input events, like React or Vue. Fill it with the @ (Create, or reuse an alias).

Expected: badge, then fills state: after filling, the state below equals the alias.

state: (empty)

4. Email-first step

Only an email field and "Continue", like the first step of Spotify's or GitHub's signup.

Expected: badge.

5. Signup in a modal dialog

The dialog is built when you click the button (content added later) and opened as a native modal, which makes the rest of the page unclickable.

Expected: badge on the dialog's email field, and the panel works inside it.

6. Field inside a Shadow DOM web component

An open shadow root with its own email input.

Expected: badge.

7. Field inside a same-origin iframe

A separate page (frame.html) from this same site, embedded in an iframe.

Expected: badge inside the iframe.

8. Newsletter box

Just type="email" and "Subscribe", no password.

Expected: badge.

9. Inside overflow: hidden

The field fills a container with overflow: hidden, so anything drawn inside it past the edges would be cut off.

Expected: badge, and the bubble's circle pokes out past the dashed box without being cut.

10. Small field and pill field

A 32px-tall field and a fully rounded one. The traced outline would look wrong on these.

Expected: badge, using the small round fallback badge, not the bubble.

Should NOT get the badge

11. Webmail-style search

A copy of Proton Mail's search box: it mentions "email address" but is a search.

Expected: no badge.

12. type="search" site search

A regular site search box.

Expected: no badge.

13. Contact form message

A textarea that mentions email in its placeholder. It's a message, not an address.

Expected: no badge.

14. Filter rule inside role="search"

An email field in a mail-filter panel marked role="search", like "Forward to email" in a webmail rule editor.

Expected: no badge.

More cases

15. Login pop-up that keeps focus (like Reddit)

A dialog inside a web component that pulls focus back to its Close button when focus leaves, centered with a transform. The username field has autocomplete="username"; there's a password field with an icon.

Expected: badge, and the panel stays open when you click the bubble.

16. Web component fields

Inputs inside components with name="username" and autocomplete="username", like Reddit's.

Expected: badge on all three. The closed one can't be read by page scripts, so check it by eye.

Email or username (slotted label)
check by eye

17. Field rendered late

Appears 2 seconds after load, the way single-page apps render forms.

Expected: badge.

18. Form inside an inline (srcdoc) iframe

Its address is about:blank, but it belongs to this site.

Expected: badge.

19. Borderless field and text newsletter field

A field with no border (fallback badge), and a type="text" newsletter field named email with an "email" label (2 + 1 = 3).

Expected: badge on both.

20. Hints that aren't enough on their own

Only a placeholder, a label or aria-labelledby mention email (1 point each), a field named emailSearch, a combobox, an email field inside class="site-search", and a read-only field.

Expected: no badge on any of them. The disabled field can't be focused, so the run skips it.

Contact email (aria-labelledby)

21. Sharing the field with another extension

A fake password-manager icon (a blue shield with class="bitwarden-inline-icon") appears at the field's right end 200ms after focus, the way Bitwarden's does.

Expected: badge, sitting right of the shield without covering it. The outline goes from Bitwarden blue, through a blend, into our blue.

22. Floating label, border on a wrapper, with another extension's icon

The input itself is borderless; the outline you see belongs to its wrapper. A Bitwarden-style shield appears 200ms after focus.

Expected: badge, with the outline traced around the wrapper (not the plain badge), sitting right of the shield without covering it.

Floating-label email

23. Small field with another extension's icon

A 32px field gets the plain badge. The shield appears 200ms after focus.

Expected: badge inside the field, 6px or more left of the shield, with typed text kept clear of it.

24. Required field with an error beside it, and another extension's icon

Leaving it empty shows a red "Email is required" to its right. A Bitwarden-style shield appears 200ms after focus.

Expected: badge inside the field, left of the shield. The bubble must not go past the shield onto the error.

Email is required

25-26. Login pop-up drawn as separate layers

Like x.com and Reddit: the dark backdrop and the white panel are painted boxes beside the form, not around it. A Bitwarden-style shield appears 200ms after focus.

Expected: 25 badge as the bubble past the shield (it fits on the panel). 26 badge inside the field, because the bubble would hang over the panel's right edge.
