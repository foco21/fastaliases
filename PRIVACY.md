# Privacy Policy: Aliases for Fastmail

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Aliases for Fastmail is a browser extension that creates Fastmail Masked Email addresses and fills them into forms. It is an independent project, not affiliated with Fastmail.

## What the extension stores

- **Your Fastmail API token.** You paste it into the settings page. It is stored only in this browser, in the extension's private storage. It is never synced to other devices and never shown again after you save it.
- **A short-lived list of your masked email addresses**, kept in memory for about a minute so the extension doesn't ask Fastmail on every click. It disappears when the browser closes or the extension restarts.

## Where data goes

- The extension talks to one server only: **api.fastmail.com**, using your token, to list, create, turn on, turn off and delete your masked email addresses.
- Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. The extension has no analytics, no tracking, no ads and no remote code. The fonts and images it uses are bundled inside it.

## Web pages you visit

To show its button, the extension looks at email fields on the pages you visit. It does not read, store or send page content. When you create or reuse an alias, it fills that address into the field and copies it to your clipboard. Web pages are only given aliases that were made for that same site.

## Sharing and selling

Your data is not sold, shared or transferred to anyone, and is not used for anything other than creating and filling masked email addresses.

## Removing your data

Remove the token on the extension's settings page, or uninstall the extension, which deletes everything it stored. To revoke access entirely, delete the token in Fastmail under Settings → Privacy & Security → Integrations → API tokens.
